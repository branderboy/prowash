The Mailchimp Killer

Every month, local franchises fall into the trap of paying upwards of $300/month to companies like Mailchimp just for the privilege of emailing customers they already earned. If you stop paying them tomorrow, you lose your audience. That means you don't own your business—they do.

We are replacing this with a fully private, self-hosted email marketing platform capable of blasting 50,000 emails a month for roughly $20. You own the database, the lists, and the infrastructure.
